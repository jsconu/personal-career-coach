# Personal Career Coach

Every Friday, your coaching agent reads the email, chat, and meeting transcripts you’ve approved and updates the same private dashboard. You can follow the evidence behind each rating and see what changed from the previous week. Pick a role or let the coach infer one, agree on what good performance looks like, and get one concrete thing to practice next week.

## Start here

1. **Install or paste.** In Claude, upload the skill ZIP in the Skills area and enable it. For an assistant that accepts text instructions, open the Markdown link above, copy the instructions, and paste them into a private chat. [Claude’s skill upload instructions](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude)
2. **Say “Set up my Personal Career Coach.”** Choose whether to describe your job or let the coach infer it from work samples. Connect the email, work chat, and transcript sources you want it to use. Together they show what happened in the meeting, what changed afterward, and what you followed through on. You can start with a single pasted excerpt.
3. **Approve your rubric.** Bring a job description or employer rubric if you have one. With web access, the coach researches public role benchmarks and explains how they apply. Choose your current role, a target role, or both.
4. **Set up Friday updates.** Choose a private dashboard your assistant can edit and confirm your connector scope and time zone. The suggested time is Friday at 3 p.m. local time. The agent saves the schedule when supported and shows the next run. Ask for a preview to see your first dashboard immediately.

## What happens on Friday?

The agent collects new work from your approved connectors, updates this week's ratings in the same dashboard, and keeps earlier weeks for comparison. You get a short private update with the dashboard link and one thing to practice. If a connector is disconnected, the dashboard shows the gap. Past scores keep their original dates.

Automatic updates require a scheduler with access to your selected connectors and private dashboard. Installing the ZIP starts the setup conversation when you invoke it. Activate the recurring job in the assistant where your work is connected. In Claude Cowork, use a recurring scheduled task. In ChatGPT agent, you can set a completed run to repeat weekly. [Claude Cowork scheduling](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork) · [ChatGPT agent scheduling](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11752874-chatgpt-agent)

**Starter prompt:** “Set up my Personal Career Coach to update the same private dashboard every Friday using my approved email, chat, and meeting transcript connectors. Show me the dashboard and the next scheduled run.”

For a quick trial with one meeting, paste a transcript you can share, identify your speaker label, and ask: **“Find one moment where I made the next step clear and one where I left someone guessing. What could I say differently?”**

## What a review looks like

**Fictional example · Customer success manager · May 4–10.** Goal: leave customer conversations with clearer next steps. Sources reviewed: customer call transcript, internal chat, and follow-up email. The person skipped video coaching.

| Category | This week | Apr 27–May 3 | Evidence and coaching |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer discovery | 3 · Meeting | 2 · Developing | May 5 call, turn 14. Your question about the rollout uncovered a training dependency. Ask it before agreeing on a date. |
| Renewal risk management | 2 · Developing | 2 · Developing | May 6 account thread and May 8 follow-up. You recorded the adoption risk, but the follow-up still asked who would own recovery. Get a name before closing the discussion. |
| Executive presence · Communication | 3 · Meeting | 3 · Meeting | May 7 email. You presented two options and asked the sponsor to select one by Friday. Reuse that structure for decisions with a deadline. |
| Executive presence · Body language | Not assessed | Not assessed | Video coaching was switched off. |
| Commercial judgment | Insufficient evidence | 3 · Meeting | The selected material contained no pricing or contract decision. |

**Next experiment:** At the end of your next at-risk account call, ask “Who owns the first recovery action, and when should we check back?” Look for an owner and date in the recap.

**A different role · Software engineer.** In a fictional design review, a senior engineer recommends a staged rollout and explains its downside. The rubric can credit technical judgment from that discussion and assess operational follow-through separately when a later handoff identifies who will monitor rollback criteria.

## Executive presence and privacy

Communication coaching covers wording and structure in text. Audio can add feedback on delivery. Body-language coaching is optional and requires a viewable clip you have permission to share. For example, the coach can identify the moment you point to a chart and whether the chart is visible as you explain it. You can correct a rating or supply missing context at any time.

The download contains instructions. Connections, browsing, video analysis, storage, and scheduling depend on the assistant where you run it. Keep source material and reports in your own approved workspace. Use fictional or redacted examples for public feedback.
